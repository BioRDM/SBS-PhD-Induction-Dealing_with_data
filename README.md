
# "Dealing with data" workshop materials
This page contains the materials used to teach students of the School of Biological Sciences at The University of Edinburgh on practical aspects of research data management.

The “Dealing with Data” workshop introduces students to Open Science and FAIR principles and equips them with effective strategies and practical tools for responsible data management throughout the research data life cycle — from data collection to sharing.
The knowledge and skills acquired help students make their research data as reusable and interoperable as possible, facilitating data sharing and the long-term preservation of their data at the end of their projects.
